Screening Standard

The 7-Point Captain Verification Standard

Only the top 15% of applicant drivers qualify to become certified VeloMoto captains. Our comprehensive onboarding funnel includes:

Govt Aadhaar & ID e-KYC
Real-time biometric and identity authentication cross-checked against government databases.
Commercial License Check
Verification of valid RTO commercial transport endorsement with zero past suspension history.
Police Criminal Verification
Stringent criminal background checks to ensure zero past record of reckless driving or misconduct.
Physical Vehicle Fitness Audit
Comprehensive 24-point vehicle inspection: brakes, dual mirrors, tire tread depth, horn, and indicators.
Trip Verification

4-Digit Start Ride OTP Verification

To guarantee you always board the correct bike taxi and captain, a unique 4-digit numeric OTP is generated upon booking. The captain's mobile meter cannot activate until you physically share this code at the start of your journey. This eliminates ride swapping and unauthorized trip startups.

Hygiene & Protection

Sanitized Dual-Visor Helmets & Hygiene Caps

Every VeloMoto bike is fitted with a rear passenger helmet certified to IS 4151 standards. Features include:

  • Anti-Scratch Clear Visor: High-clarity wind and dust shield for eye protection.
  • Daily UV & Antibacterial Sanitization: Helmets are sanitized before every morning and evening shift.
  • Free Disposable Hairnets: Sealed non-woven hygienic caps provided to every passenger before boarding.
Live Monitoring

Real-Time GPS Tracking & Share Trip Live

From pickup to destination drop-off, your ride is tracked live by satellite at 2-second intervals. With one tap on the "Share Trip" button, you can send an encrypted web link to friends or family via WhatsApp or SMS, allowing them to follow your bike's live location, captain details, and estimated time of arrival in real time.

Emergency Response

24x7 Rapid Action SOS & Emergency Protocol

The VeloMoto app features an ever-present, one-tap emergency SOS button during all active rides. Triggering SOS initiates three simultaneous protocols:

Police Direct Connect Immediate connection to national emergency 112 with live coordinates transmitted.
VeloMoto Safety Desk Immediate outbound call from our 24x7 crisis response specialists.
Emergency Contacts Automated SMS alert with your exact GPS pin dispatched to your trusted contacts.
Identity Protection

Two-Way Private Calling (Number Masking)

Your private mobile number is never disclosed to captains, and their phone numbers are never disclosed to you. All pre-ride pickup calls route through our secure virtual telecom exchange, ensuring zero unwanted follow-up contact once your ride is concluded.

Financial Safety

₹5 Lakh Complimentary Accident Coverage

Safety includes financial peace of mind. Every single passenger riding with VeloMoto is automatically insured under our group accidental insurance policy for up to ₹5,00,000 against accidental disability or loss of life, and up to ₹1,00,000 for emergency medical hospitalization expenses.

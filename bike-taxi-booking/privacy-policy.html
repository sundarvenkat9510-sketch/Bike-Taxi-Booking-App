Data Protection & Privacy

VeloMoto Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026. Your privacy, real-time trip confidentiality, and digital security are fundamental to every journey you take with VeloMoto.

Table of Contents
1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Data 3. Location & GPS Tracking 4. Phone Number Masking 5. Payment & Financial Data 6. Data Sharing & Partners 7. Security & Encryption 8. User Rights & Deletion 9. Grievance Officer
Questions? Contact privacy@velomoto.com
Our Privacy Pledge: VeloMoto never sells, rents, or monetizes your personal data or travel history to third-party advertisers or data brokers. All personal information is strictly used for ride dispatching, safety verification, and transparent trip billing.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or ride with VeloMoto Technologies Inc. ("VeloMoto", "we", "us", or "our"), we collect relevant information necessary to deliver seamless bike taxi transportation services:

  • Account Information: Full legal name, mobile phone number, email address, and profile photo when you register.
  • Location Data: High-precision GPS coordinates during trip booking, pickup coordination, active ride progress, and destination drop-off.
  • Transaction & Payment Details: Ride fare amounts, chosen payment method (UPI, cash, wallet), date/time of transactions, and promotional voucher codes redeemed.
  • Device & App Telemetry: Device hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and app crash logs for performance enhancement.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data powers fast, reliable, and secure daily commutes:

  • Captain Matching: Calculating real-time proximity to dispatch the nearest verified captain in under 2 minutes.
  • Optimal Route Navigation: Guiding captains through live traffic bypass corridors to beat congestion.
  • Safety Monitoring: Detecting unusual route deviations or sudden prolonged stops during active rides.
  • Customer Support & Receipts: Issuing itemized digital tax invoices and resolving ride feedback or lost property queries.

3. Location Tracking & Background Permissions

We respect your battery and location privacy:

VeloMoto accesses location data exclusively while the app is actively in use or during an ongoing trip. As soon as your ride is completed and the trip is closed by your captain, high-precision background GPS telemetry is immediately terminated. You may revoke location permissions through your device settings anytime, though active location is required to request bike taxis.

4. Phone Number Masking & Private Calling

To protect rider and captain personal privacy, VeloMoto utilizes virtual cloud PBX number masking. When you call your captain to coordinate pickup, neither party's actual telephone number is exposed. All coordination calls are routed through secure virtual proxy numbers.

5. Payment & Financial Data

All digital transactions on VeloMoto are routed through Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment aggregators (Razorpay / PayU / Cashfree). VeloMoto never stores your debit/credit card numbers, CVV codes, bank login credentials, or UPI PINs on our servers.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Compliance

We share data only when strictly necessary:

  • With Your Assigned Captain: Pickup location, drop destination, and your first name. Captains never receive your email or billing history.
  • With Insurance Partners: Trip identifiers and timestamp records required to process accidental claims under our complimentary ₹5,00,000 rider insurance policy.
  • Legal & Law Enforcement: Only upon receipt of a valid, authorized warrant or statutory request under the Information Technology Act.

7. Data Security & Encryption

We employ industry-leading 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all communications between your mobile device and our secure cloud infrastructure hosted in ISO/IEC 27001 certified data centers.

8. User Rights & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your digital identity. You have the right to request a complete copy of your ride data or request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal identifiers by visiting your profile settings or emailing privacy@velomoto.com. Account deletion requests are permanently finalized within 7 business days.

Standard Operating Procedure

The VeloMoto Safety Protocol

How we ensure every single meter of your journey remains protected, hygienic, and smooth.

1. Identity Match

Always verify that your Captain's bike registration plate and face match the details in your app.

2. Sanitized Helmet

Your Captain will hand you an ISI-certified helmet and disposable hair cap before starting the ride.

3. Share OTP

Share your 4-digit ride OTP only after safely boarding the bike and wearing your helmet.

4. Live GPS Guard

Our 24/7 central control center monitors any abnormal route deviation or unscheduled stop automatically.

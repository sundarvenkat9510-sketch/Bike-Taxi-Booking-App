Our Mission & Vision

Empowering commuters, supporting rider livelihoods, and greening Indian transit.

Our Mission

To democratize intra-city urban mobility by offering the fastest, most economical, and reliable point-to-point bike taxi rides. We aim to make daily commutes hassle-free for everyone — from metro commuters to tech workers, college students, and daily shoppers.

  • Sub-3-minute pickup wait times in urban centers
  • Zero surge pricing spikes on bad weather or peak hours
  • Safe, sanitized, and government compliant transit

Our Vision

To build a smarter, zero-congestion urban mobility ecosystem that bridges first-and-last-mile public transit gaps across Indian metropolises, while accelerating the country's transition toward clean, quiet, and electric two-wheeler commuting.

  • Rapid EV fleet expansion to slash vehicular emissions
  • Sustainable flexible livelihood for over 200,000 Captains
  • Seamless multimodal integration with Metro & Rail hubs
What Drives Us

The VeloMoto Core Pillars

Four non-negotiable principles that shape every single ride we power.

Safety Above All

Comprehensive background verification, sanitized ISI helmets, 24x7 live SOS tracking, and ₹5 Lakh passenger transit insurance on every booking.

Transparent Fares

No hidden surcharges or peak multipliers. You always see the upfront exact fare calculated with honest per-km meter rates before you step on.

Captain Empowerment

Our Captains are the heart of VeloMoto. We provide industry-lowest commissions, same-day payouts, and financial health security.

Green Transit

Actively transitioning toward 100% Electric Vehicles (EVs) to curb city carbon footprints, eliminate smoke, and cut noise pollution.

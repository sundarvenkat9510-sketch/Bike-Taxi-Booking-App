2. The VeloMoto Platform & Intermediary Role

VeloMoto provides a digital technology intermediary platform that matches independent licensed commercial motorcycle and electric scooter operators ("Captains") with passengers seeking urban transportation. VeloMoto itself does not own the vehicles or employ captains as traditional transport drivers; captains operate as verified independent mobility partners adhering to strict VeloMoto service standards.

3. User Eligibility & Account Responsibility

  • You must be at least 18 years of age to register an independent rider account.
  • You agree to provide accurate, truthful, and updated contact credentials during registration.
  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all rides dispatched under your profile.

4. Mandatory Helmet Policy & Life Safety

Under the Motor Vehicles Amendment Act, wearing a protective helmet is compulsory for both the motorcycle rider and pillion passenger. VeloMoto captains carry a sanitized, dual-visor, ISI-certified passenger helmet along with disposable hygiene hairnets for your comfort.

Captain's Right of Refusal: If a passenger refuses to wear the provided protective helmet, the Captain reserves the absolute statutory right to cancel the trip immediately. No cancellation penalty will be levied against the captain in such instances.

5. Upfront Fares & Transparent Metering

  • Base Fare: Standard base pickup fare applies depending on the ride tier selected (Standard Moto, Eco Electric, or Express Pro).
  • Per-Kilometer & Duration: Transparent distance rate calculated via GPS distance tracking, plus nominal waiting duration fees if applicable.
  • Tolls & Parking: Any municipal bridge tolls or airport entry charges requested by the passenger during transit are billed transparently on the final invoice.
  • Surge-Free Guarantee: VeloMoto strives for transparent metered pricing without arbitrary hidden surge multipliers.

6. Cancellations & Waiting Fees

You may cancel a booked ride free of charge at any time before captain dispatch or within 2 minutes of a captain accepting your request. If cancellation occurs after 2 minutes or after the captain has already arrived at the designated pickup location, a nominal cancellation fee (₹20 - ₹35) will be charged to compensate the captain for fuel and transit time.

7. Passenger Code of Conduct

To maintain a safe community, passengers must adhere to the following rules:

  • Treat captains with mutual respect and courtesy. Zero tolerance for verbal abuse or harassment.
  • Luggage is restricted to one standard shoulder backpack or bag not exceeding 10 kg that can be safely worn by the passenger without obstructing bike handling.
  • Transporting contraband, alcohol, flammable liquids, hazardous chemicals, or prohibited substances is strictly forbidden and results in immediate account debarment and police reporting.

8. Complimentary Accidental Insurance

Every booked VeloMoto trip is automatically covered under our comprehensive group accidental insurance policy at zero additional charge. Coverage includes up to ₹5,00,000 for accidental disability or demise and up to ₹1,00,000 for emergency hospitalization and medical expenses directly resulting from an in-transit collision. Claims are processed via our licensed underwriting partners.

9. Limitation of Liability

VeloMoto exercises diligent background screening and rigorous vehicle inspection protocols. However, VeloMoto shall not be held liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from traffic congestion delays, adverse weather conditions, or unforeseen road closures beyond our reasonable operational control.
